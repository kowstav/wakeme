# Welcome revision — 31 October 2026

## Owner feedback and scope

The supplied Android screenshot has an isolated wordmark, a boxed illustration, a heavy centred heading and an unaligned third sign-in action. The owner requested direct visual references rather than another generic polish pass.

This revision replaces the welcome composition on Android and iOS. Account, consent, navigation and alarm behavior remain the existing native flows. This does not mark the wider product or release gates complete.

## References actually viewed

- [DreamFlow by Tetiana Yaremenko](https://dribbble.com/shots/26697981-Onboarding-for-sleep-treaking-app): viewed the actual three-screen image through the browser. Adopted the continuous environmental backdrop and concise text hierarchy; did not reuse the photographs or introduce its tracking claims.
- [Onboarding Sleep by M_Kado](https://dribbble.com/shots/12275177-Onboarding-Sleep-app): viewed the actual three-screen image through the browser. Adopted the integrated illustration field and rounded lower action panel. Retained WakeMe's original art and semantic colors rather than its purple palette or floating characters.

- [Calm Sleep by Priyanka](https://dribbble.com/shots/22005434-Calm-Sleep-Onboarding-Experience): viewed its actual three-phone image. Adopted the clear account action below a compact headline and environmental illustration, without its unrelated music features or artwork.

These are specific visual references, not evidence that their concepts shipped or represent all current apps. The previously generated original illustration is retained, but its boxed container is removed from welcome. No third-party artwork is bundled.

## Native implementation

- Remove the separate top wordmark.
- Use one full-width morning scene flowing into a rounded lower surface.
- Align the heading and supporting line to the same 28-point inset as the actions.
- Use a 30-point medium-weight heading with lighter tracking, rather than the global bold centred display style. Native accessibility text scaling remains enabled.
- Google remains the direct Android account action. Replace the vague extra link with a centred **Continue with email** action immediately below it.
- Keep local alarm setup as a separate text action after a subtle divider; preserve its existing accessible label and navigation.
- iOS uses its existing platform account entry.
- Scroll the whole composition on short windows, instead of splitting it into a story and a disconnected pinned footer. Reduce decorative image height as text size grows.
- Add actual Compose renders for 320×640, dark and 1.6× text alongside existing phone/foldable/tablet/desktop coverage.
