Bloom

Wake Me's design system after the October 2026 review of top US apps (docs/design/research-2026-10-21-top-apps.md). White canvas, flat grey cards, lime actions with forest ink, and petal colours for things of the same kind. Every value here comes from packages/design-tokens/tokens.json and icons/*.svg; this page is a study, the apps are the product. It is not a render of the native apps.

Screens (light, and the home in dark)

Where icons, words, or both

Icon onlyActions everyone recognises, in compact places: back, more, close, play a preview, send in the composer. Always with a spoken label.
Icon and a short wordNavigation tabs and quick actions (Messages, Check in, Location, Suggest, Safety). The word stays visible; the icon makes the row scannable.
Words, with a leading iconPrimary and secondary buttons where the icon adds recognition (Send quack, Invite, New wake-up, End this routine).
Words onlyConsent and permission decisions, dialog buttons, alarm Stop and every escape: nothing may be ambiguous there.
