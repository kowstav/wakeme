# Owner review and UX laws pass (2026-10-23)

Source: the owner's annotated review (`This part doesn.pdf`, two pages) and a request to apply 20 UX laws to
the whole app. Both platforms changed together. Nothing here has been compiled or rendered yet; CI will be
the first build.

## The owner's review

| Point | Change |
|---|---|
| The friend page's Messages, Suggest and Safety circles don't line up with the avatar. | The header follows the Contacts layout. A 96 pt face is centred, the name sits under it, and the actions sit in fixed 76 pt columns centred on the same axis. |
| Lower the opacity of those circles. | Quick-action fills use the family colour at 50 %, on both platforms and on the circle page. The lime primary is unchanged. |
| Profile pictures: animal cartoon faces instead of letters. 10 animals, different colours, funny. | `packages/design-tokens/faces`: cat, cow, dog, donkey, frog, monkey, owl, pig, rooster and sheep, plus a duck for the sound picker. Each has its own background and a gag (tongue out, buck teeth, cross-eyed, mismatched eyes, crowing). The generator builds them into Android vector drawables (circles and ellipses become paths) and full-colour iOS imagesets, and generates `WMFace` on both platforms. A friend's face comes from a stable hash of their id, the same function and order on both platforms. |
| The notes look misaligned, say too much, and the icon doesn't line up with the text. | `Note` is now a plain caption with no icon (an icon next to wrapped text never lines up). Almost every note was removed (see below). Current guidance says helper text belongs only where a control isn't obvious, short and next to it; a control that needs a paragraph usually has the wrong label. |
| Many animal sounds, not only the quack; quack is the default; no announcement that you can choose. | Ten new calls, synthesised in `packages/sounds` like the existing set: ribbit, woof, meow, moo, ooh-ooh, hee-haw, oink, baa, hoot and cock-a-doodle. No samples and no third-party rights. They are in `ANIMAL_SOUNDS` and `BUNDLED_SOUNDS` (policy), the "Gentle nudges" preset (quack first), every app sound map and Android's notification channels. On a friend's page the allowed animals show as a row of faces: tapping one selects it and plays it on your phone, and the button follows ("Send woof"). Nothing explains this. |
| The duck shouldn't be the centre of the app. | The welcome hero is a huddle of five friends' faces, each playing its own call when tapped. The duck no longer appears on the friend page or in empty states (they use a face). It stays only as the quack sound's face and in the app-switcher cover. |
| The design is explicit everywhere. Store users already know how apps work. | Roughly 70 sentences of explanation were removed or cut to a few words across both apps (list below). What stays is state (what is happening now), consequences in confirmation dialogs for destructive actions, and the few safety and legal lines: the emergency-number line, now under the SOS button; location capture time and accuracy; the Play accessibility disclosure. |

### Copy that went

- **iOS:** welcome privacy line; guest intro and the "Wake each other up" line; readiness intro, denied help text and test-alarm description; all three Focus footers and the focus-request line; both circle footers; Challenges intro, publish and version explanations, the escape reassurance and the AI-credits line (shortened); the invite note and join-code help; the friend page's ownership note, "turned off wake alarms" note and suggestion footnote; every permission-editor footer and the intro note; the sign-in reason; the time-zone explanation (now a row); the check-in and location explanations (shortened); the Safety intro (moved to the SOS footer); "Your own alarms still ring…".
- **Android:** the same set, plus the invite-preview reasons; Focus shield copy; voice-sound explanations; routine-editor explanations; You-screen pause, quiet-hours, sound-budget and primary-phone explanations (cut to a few words); readiness step sentences (now labels such as "Notifications" or "Alarms & reminders"); the alarm-screen state lines (shortened).

## The 20 laws

| Law | Where it shows in the app now |
|---|---|
| 1. Aesthetic-usability effect | Animal faces, aligned centred header, softer action circles, no misaligned notes, a celebratory finish. |
| 2. Hick's law | Permission editor: on/off switches and faces first; hours and limits behind "Hours and limits" / "Limits" (fewer visible choices). Quack card: one row of faces and one button. |
| 3. Fitts's law | Primary actions full width or pinned at the bottom (welcome, guest home). Quick actions in 76 pt columns. Readiness rows put "Open settings" at the end of the row, in thumb reach. Every face chip is 68 pt wide. |
| 4. Jakob's law | Friend header copies the system Contacts card. Native `DisclosureGroup` (iOS) and an equivalent row with a turning chevron (Android). Swipe to delete, the share sheet and system pickers unchanged. |
| 5. Miller's law | Invite code shown and accepted in 3-3-3 chunks. Templates in pairs. Sounds in a short row (allowed ones only) or a grid. Readiness as two steps. |
| 6. Doherty threshold | Tapping a face plays its sound and ticks a haptic at once. Android "Loading…" text became an animated indicator (`Loading`). Send buttons switch to "Sending…" immediately. |
| 7. Law of proximity | Captions removed or placed directly under the control they qualify. Actions sit directly under the face and name. Each readiness step is one row. |
| 8. Law of similarity | One `SoundChoice` look for picking (friend page) and allowing (permissions). All quick actions share a size and a 50 % fill. Readiness steps share one row pattern. |
| 9. Common region | Sound faces and the send button share one card. Readiness steps share one card (no cards inside cards). Members share one card on Android. |
| 10. Visual hierarchy | One display headline per hero moment. Face, then name, then actions. Card titles over captions. |
| 11. Progressive disclosure | Permission detail behind disclosure rows. Explanations removed rather than shown up front. Wake-alarm and quack cards appear only when they can act. |
| 12. Recognition over recall | Sounds are faces you see and hear, not names in a list. Presets ("Gentle nudges", "Wake buddy") instead of building rules from scratch. Readiness labels name the actual setting. |
| 13. Von Restorff effect | One lime button per screen. On a friend's page, "Choose what they can do" turns secondary when there's a quack to send. SOS is the only red button. |
| 14. Peak-end rule | A finished wake-up ends on "You're up." in display type, with a hopping, calling face and a success haptic (iOS `YoureUpView`, Android `AlarmActivity`). |
| 15. Goal-gradient effect | `StepProgress` on readiness (two segments) and in the challenge runner. The bar fills as each step is done. |
| 16. Serial position effect | Messages first and Safety last in action rows. Challenges: Create, then yours, then templates. The welcome's lime "I have an invite" comes first. |
| 17. Tesler's law | The app absorbs the complexity. Every animal allowed by default (quack first). The time zone is detected and shown, not explained. Join accepts any code shape. The invite link is shared, not shown. |
| 18. Postel's law | `InviteInput` (both platforms) accepts "s9p kr4 ezg", "S9PKR4EZG", en dashes, or a whole pasted link (its token is used). Unit tests are in `InviteInputTest.kt`. |
| 19. Occam's razor | Roughly 70 explanations removed. "Quack available now" dropped from the friend header (the card shows it). The raw invite link is no longer shown. Readiness "next step" notes were replaced by the step bar. |
| 20. Feedback loops | Face tap gives sound and haptic. Send gives "Sending…", then "Sent to Alex", the delivery trail and a haptic. Readiness bar fills. The finish screen calls back. |

## Not done, and why

- **The animal calls are synthesised cartoons.** They are new sounds with no rights issues, but nobody has listened to them yet. The owner should approve them before release; recorded CC0 or licensed calls could replace them later without code changes.
- **No native renders.** Both apps need CI for the first build and the screenshots of this pass. Previews now include the huddle, every face, the sound row and grid, the step bar, a caption and the loading indicator.
- **Kept on purpose:** confirmation-dialog consequences (block, remove, delete, leave); the account-deletion text; the SOS guidance to call the friend; location capture and accuracy; the alarm-screen audio-route state (doc 06 evidence rules).
