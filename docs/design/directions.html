Comparison

CriterionA · SunriseB · Linen & InkC · DaybreakNotes
Emotional fit (playful, warm)524B reads as a banking app; A reads as a game.
Legibility at 6 am / large text355A's coral selection and butter canvas reduce contrast headroom; rounded heavy type wraps sooner.
Trust in safety, alarms and permissions254A's coral collides with danger/SOS semantics and makes care feel frivolous (doc 03 §2).
Distinctiveness424B could be any app. C owns yellow + duck + warm night.
Cross-platform consistency255A needs a bundled rounded font on Android to match iOS's SF Rounded.
Accessibility (contrast, HC, colour-blind)355C: 42 token pairs pass in light, dark and both increased-contrast modes.
Total192427

Selected: C · Daybreak. It keeps the trusted structure the product needs for alarms, permissions and SOS, and spends colour and motion only on the moments that carry emotion: the primary action, selection, the quack and success. It is also the closest to the existing identity, so it refines rather than replaces what already passes contrast and screenshot review.
