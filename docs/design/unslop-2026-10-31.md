# First light: an unslop pass over Daylight — 31 October 2026

Applied with [unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop) by Damir Sokolovsky (CC BY 4.0), revision `4652254`. This is a refinement of Daylight (ADR 0022), recorded in [ADR 0023](../adr/0023-first-light-refinement.md). It is not a new palette: three repaints in ten days had already moved the app around, and the lavender, ink and violet read as the sky before sunrise once they are given a reason to be there.

## Direction

- **Product.** Close friends quack, nudge and wake each other, and each person decides what friends can do on their phone.
- **Character.** Warm, dependable, a little cheeky.
- **World.** The bedroom at first light: an ink room, a lavender sky, the sun about to come over the hill.
- **Moments.** The time card, an ink night with the sun on its path. The welcome scene, the same sun half risen behind the hills.
- **Signature.** The dawn arc: a thin horizon and a dotted path with the sun, or a crescent moon at night, sitting exactly where this alarm falls in the day.

The arc is drawn from the alarm's real time, so it is data rather than decoration. A 06:15 alarm is a sun barely over the horizon; 08:30 sits higher; 23:10 is a moon. It replaces a sun icon that was identical on every card.

## What did not change

Violet, lavender and ink, Manrope, the four tabs, the friend page's Contacts layout and half-strength action circles (owner's PDF review), the animal faces and sounds, the delivery trail, the owned icon set, the motion system and every token for motion. Motion was already compliant: nothing loops, and reduced motion is handled, so that pass made no changes.

No feature, route, API call, permission flow or escape was removed. Every string the Android and iOS UI tests look up was kept.
