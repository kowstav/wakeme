## 1. The service

Wake Me lets friends nudge and wake each other within limits each person sets for their own
phone, run shared wake-up routines and challenges, and send messages. [Legal entity]
provides it.

## 2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and able to agree to these terms. One account per person.

## 3. Consent is the core rule

- You may only send what the recipient allowed. The app enforces their limits; don't try to
  get around them (for example with several accounts or by pressuring someone to change
  their settings).
- Permissions belong to the person whose phone they affect. They can pause, change or remove
  them at any time, and can block you; blocking removes permissions both ways.
- Never use Wake Me to harass, coerce, stalk or frighten anyone. Location sharing is started
  only by the person sharing and ends when they stop it or the time runs out.

## 4. What Wake Me doesn't promise

- **Wake Me is not an emergency service.** Check-ins and location sharing are conveniences
  between friends, not emergency response. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
- **We can't guarantee that an alarm rings or that someone wakes up.** Phones can be off,
  silenced by the operating system, out of battery or restricted by settings. The app shows
  what your phone reported (for example "Ready on this phone"), not that a person heard it.
  Keep a backup alarm for anything important.
- A finished challenge is not proof that someone is awake.
- Wake Me is not medical treatment for sleep or other conditions.

## 5. Your content

You keep ownership of what you create (messages, challenges, sounds). You give us the
permission needed to store and deliver it to the people you choose. Only upload sounds you
have the right to use. AI-drafted challenges are checked by automatic rules before you can
use them, but you're responsible for what you send to friends.

## 6. Safety and moderation

You can report people, messages, challenges, sounds and requests. We review reports and may
remove content, suspend accounts (which revokes all their permissions) or close accounts that
break these terms. [Appeal process.]

Wake Me Plus is optional. Prices, trial length and renewal date are shown before you buy.
Subscriptions renew through Apple or Google until you cancel in your store account settings;
deleting your Wake Me account does not cancel them. A subscription never decides whether you
can stop an alarm, remove a permission or end a restriction. [Refund rules by store and region.]
