# Listing copy — DRAFT

**Name:** Wake Me
**Subtitle / short description (≤30 / ≤80 chars):** Alert your friends. Wake each other up.

**Description:**

Wake Me is for friends who help each other get up, show up and keep going.

Send a quack when you need a friend's attention. Ask a friend to wake you for that early
class. Set a wake-up together and finish a quick quiz or puzzle to start the day.

You're in control of your phone:
• Nobody can do anything on your phone until you choose what they may do.
• Set limits: which sounds, how often, which hours. Quiet hours are respected.
• Pause or remove a friend's permissions any time. Block and report in two taps.
• Your own alarms ring even when you're offline.

Made for small groups of real friends:
• Private invites, no contact upload, no phone number needed.
• Messages and reactions between friends.
• Wake-up routines friends can suggest and you accept.
• Challenges you make yourselves, with optional AI drafting.

Honest about what happened: Wake Me tells you "their phone received it" or "alarm scheduled
on their phone", never that someone is awake when it can't know.

Wake Me is not an emergency service and can't guarantee an alarm wakes anyone. For adults 18+.
