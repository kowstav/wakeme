## Focus test on the CI emulators (2026-10-21, Claude)

- **What changed** (test and CI only; the Focus service, its accessibility config and the manifest are unchanged): `FocusShieldTest` and the Focus step of `android-device.yml`, on branch `ci/focus-binding-api36` ([PR #2](https://github.com/kowstav/wakeme/pull/2)).
- **Android 16, "the system binds the focus service" (runs 55, 58, 60).** `am instrument` kills the app process, and the system keeps the service in its binding or crashed set (the accessibility dumps in runs 62–68 show both). AccessibilityManagerService skips a service in either set while it is enabled, and clears them only when its settings observer reads the setting without the service (`removeDisabledServicesFromTemporaryStatesLocked`; AOSP android16-release, android17-release is the same). The observer reads the current value, so the test's back-to-back delete and put could both look unchanged: the failing logcats show no `onUserStateChangedLocked` after the writes, passing run 56 shows one between them. **Fix:** delete the setting, wait (≤ 5 s) until `dumpsys accessibility` no longer lists the service as enabled, put it back and wait ≤ 10 s for the binding, up to three cycles. The binding assertion stays; a failure reports the enabled/binding/crashed sets, and CI keeps the accessibility dump from before and after the Focus step.
- **Android 17, "no shield after End focus" (runs 61, 64, 65).** The test finished the shield, reopened Settings 2–3 ms later and checked at once, while the closing shield was still resumed; the logcats show no shield started after End focus. **Fix:** wait (≤ 4 s) for the finished shield to close, then open the chosen app and assert that no shield appears for 4 s.
- **Verified (emulators, not device evidence):** at `c26b85a` the Android 16 Focus step passed in runs 62, 64, 65 and 68, each binding after one off/on cycle (Android 17 binding 4/4 too). At `0559807` the Focus step passed on both Android 16 and 17 in [run 69](https://github.com/kowstav/wakeme/actions/runs/39576135980) (PR #2), [70](https://github.com/kowstav/wakeme/actions/runs/39577956224) and [71](https://github.com/kowstav/wakeme/actions/runs/39579688303), each binding after one off/on cycle; run 70 passed every job. Android 16 Focus step: 7 consecutive passes (runs 62–71).
- **Not run:** no Android SDK on this machine; the test compiled and ran only in CI. Nothing on a physical phone.
- **Still open:** `QuickActionsWidgetTest` ("Choose a friend to quack" not found; runs 61 and 65 on both emulators, Android 17 in 55 and 58) fails the instrumented-tests step independently of Focus (again in run 69, Android 16). In run 71 the Android 17 journey skipped itself ("dev backend not reachable", socket timeout), which the step counts as a failure.

## Open decisions and deviations

- `message.send` is not a grant: accepted friends can message each other; block ends the conversation.
- `location.last_known.read` became `location.request`: no standing location read; every share is owner-started and time-boxed.
- Re-authentication window for high-impact grants is 24 h by default.
- Android uses `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` (user-granted), not `USE_EXACT_ALARM`, pending a Play policy decision.
- Only the primary device installs and rings routines; other devices remove local copies on sync. Signing out the primary promotes the most recently seen device.
- Routines are armed 8 days ahead (at most 8 occurrences). A phone that never runs Wake Me for 8 days stops ringing them, and friends see "not yet confirmed". On iPhone, a routine without a challenge has no follow-up (nothing on the alarm could cancel it).
- Retention values (30 days request metadata and AI prompts, 45 days sync feed, 365 days audit) are proposals pending legal review.
- Location shares send a fix only when the OS delivers one (Android: every 60 s or 25 m; iOS: live updates, which pause while the phone is still). A phone left still may send nothing new, so friends see the true capture time and, after 10 minutes, a warning. Whether to resend an unchanged position with a fresh time needs real-device observation first.
- **Doc 05 skills**: Google Android skills are pinned at ed0a272. The Codex continuation uses seven relevant skills (reviewed in `docs/android-skills-review.md`); the earlier repo review is in `.claude/skills/REVIEW.md`. No additional iOS or security skill installation is part of this Android continuation.
- Full apps were built before the Stage 0 device experiments (doc 04 §2) could run, because no physical devices are available to this work; the Stage 0 exit gate is still open and its results may change promises.
- Android instrumented tests use Espresso 3.8.0-alpha01 (test-only, never shipped): the first release that injects input on Android 17. Move to the stable release when it ships.
